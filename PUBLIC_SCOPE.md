# Public Scope

## Purpose

This repository demonstrates practical thinking about enterprise AI enablement, workflow improvement, governance, adoption, and operational readiness.

## Evidence Boundary

- All examples are generic or fictional.
- No outcome is presented as a client, employer, or production result.
- No metric is claimed unless it is explicitly framed as a proposed measure.
- Templates are decision aids, not legal, security, privacy, or compliance advice.

## Information Excluded

This repository intentionally excludes:

- confidential client or employer information;
- private business architecture, repositories, systems, routes, and environments;
- credentials, secrets, account identifiers, and configuration values;
- internal prompts, operating instructions, logs, and security controls;
- personal, financial, trading, or sensitive operational data.

## Safe Reuse

Before adapting these materials, an organisation should apply its own security, privacy, legal, records-management, procurement, and change-management requirements. Named owners should approve data access, pilot scope, operational use, and scale-up decisions.
